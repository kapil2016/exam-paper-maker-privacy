Privacy Policy

Exam Paper Maker is made by appcraftstudio. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short, because the app is built to need very little.

There is no account

The app has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name, your email address, your phone number, your location or your contacts, and we have no way to learn them. Nothing you do in the app is tied to your identity, because we do not have it.

Photographs you ask the app to read

When you press Read, the photographs you selected are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to Google's Gemini service. Gemini converts the questions in the picture into text and sends the text back. That is the only thing the photograph is used for.

  • The photograph is not stored on our server, before or after reading.
  • It is not used to train any model.
  • Photographs you have not asked the app to read never leave your phone.

Google's handling of the image is governed by the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our server runs on Cloudflare Workers, so Cloudflare carries the traffic; it is covered by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Your question papers stay on your phone

The papers you make, the questions in them, the school and class details you type, and the PDFs you export are stored on your device only. We cannot see them and they are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them, so export anything you want to keep before you do.

What our server keeps

Only what is needed to count the pages you have used and honour what you have bought.

A random identifier Created the first time you open the app. It is a random number, not linked to you and not an advertising identifier. It is how the server knows which balance is yours without knowing who you are.
An install ID A one-way hash (SHA-256) of the device identifier Android gives this app. It lets the server give your own balance back after a reinstall and stops the free pages being claimed over and over. It cannot be turned back into that identifier and is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
Your page balance How many pages you have left, and how many were free or bought.
A fingerprint of each page already read A one-way hash of the image, so that reading the same photograph again is never charged twice. It cannot be turned back into the picture, and the picture itself is not kept.
Purchases The order reference Google Play gives us, so a purchase can be credited once and restored if you change phone.
Free pages from ads For each free page earned by watching an ad: the transaction ID AdMob gives the reward, the ad unit and the date, so a reward is added once and the daily limit can be kept.
Daily totals How many pages the service read each day, across everybody. Just numbers — nothing that points at a person.

We do not store your network address. When the app first registers, your address is used to work out how many free pages to give and is immediately turned into a one-way fingerprint that cannot be reversed into an address. That fingerprint is deleted the following day.

Payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, your UPI ID or your billing address — only Google's confirmation that a purchase happened, so we can add the pages. Refunds and payment problems are handled by Google Play under its own privacy policy.

Optional video ads for free pages

On the Pages screen you can choose to watch a short video ad to earn a free page, a few times a day. Ads are shown only when you tap Watch ad; the app shows no other ads.

  • The ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested and shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, ad interaction data (for example, whether the ad was watched to the end) and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google uses it to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads — see How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • So the free page reaches the right balance, the app passes your install ID with the ad request, and AdMob sends it back to our server with a transaction ID when you finish the ad. We do not receive your advertising ID.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and Settings → Privacy choices for ads lets you change it. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Two questions we ask

The first time you open the app it asks who will use it (for example, school teacher or student) and what you want it to do, and when your free pages run out it asks what would make a pack worth buying. Both can be skipped. Your answers, and anything you choose to type, are sent to our server with the install ID described above and the app's version, and are used only to decide what to improve. Please don't type personal details in the optional text box.

How the app is used (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which parts of the app work and where people get stuck, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records which screens are opened and steps such as opening the camera, reading pages, viewing the page packs, tapping Buy or Watch ad, and exporting a paper — with counts like how many pages were read — together with your answers to the two questions above. It never records the content of your photographs or papers.

Firebase collects this with an app-instance identifier and, where your phone allows it, the advertising ID, along with device information such as model, operating system, language and approximate location derived from the IP address. We have switched off the use of this data for ad personalisation. Google's use of it is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

The app is made for teachers and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Don't watch ads — they are only shown when you tap Watch ad.
  • Skip the questions — both have a Skip.
  • Delete everything on your phone — uninstall the app.
  • Delete what our server holds — email us and we will remove your identifier, your balance and your page fingerprints. Note that this also removes any pages you have bought and not yet used.
  • Ask what we hold — email us and we will tell you.
